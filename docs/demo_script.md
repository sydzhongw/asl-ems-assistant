# Demo Script (2:00)

**Cast:** EMT, Patient (signs), Voiceover (narrator)
**Rule:** only use phrases and signs that are in `data/lexicon.json` and work reliably on the phone.

## 0:00-0:15 | Hook
- **Visual:** Patient on the couch holding their chest. EMT walks in. They look at each other, and the EMT gestures helplessly.
- **Voiceover:** "When a Deaf patient has a medical emergency, the paramedic often can't sign. Many culturally Deaf people use ASL as their first language and may not read English easily, so writing notes doesn't work."

## 0:15-0:30 | The app
- **Visual:** EMT takes out a phone and opens the app. Quick shot of the home screen, then the call screen.
- **Voiceover:** "We built an ASL translator for EMS. It translates. It never asks questions or gives medical advice."

## 0:30-0:55 | English → ASL
- **Visual:** Split screen with EMT on one side and the phone screen on the other.
- **EMT says:** "Where does it hurt?"
- **App:** the signer clip plays. Patient nods and points to their chest.
- **Voiceover:** "The EMT speaks. The AI matches it to an approved EMS phrase, and a real signer plays back."

## 0:55-1:20 | ASL → English
- **Visual:** Patient faces the phone camera and signs CHEST + PAIN.
- **App:** shows "My chest hurts" with a "Did you mean...?" confirm button. EMT taps confirm. The text is read aloud.
- **Voiceover:** "The patient signs. The app reads the signs and builds a sentence, and the EMT confirms before anything is trusted."

## 1:20-1:40 | Two-way exchange
- **EMT says:** "Do you have any allergies?" The clip plays.
- **Patient signs:** NO. The app shows "No allergies."
- **Voiceover:** "Back and forth, in seconds."

## 1:40-2:00 | Close
- **Visual:** Architecture diagram for about 5 seconds, then the team and tool logos (Gemini, Cursor, Entire).
- **Voiceover:** "Gemini powers the translation. Real signers, confidence checks, and a path to a certified interpreter keep it safe. Faster, clearer care when seconds matter."
